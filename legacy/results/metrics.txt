Metric definitions (positive class = Abnormal)
  Accuracy    : overall percentage of correct predictions.
  Precision   : percentage of predicted-Abnormal images that are truly Abnormal.
  Sensitivity : (recall) ability to correctly identify abnormal cases, TP/(TP+FN).
  Specificity : ability to correctly identify normal cases, TN/(TN+FP).
  F1-score    : harmonic mean (balance) of precision and recall.
  ROC AUC     : probability that a random Abnormal image gets a higher
                score than a random Normal image (0.5 = chance).
